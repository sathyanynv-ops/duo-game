How to play DUO

Each player starts with 7 cards. On your turn play one card that matches the top card's color or number/symbol. Wilds go on anything. First to empty their hand wins.

Can't play?

  • Tap the deck to draw one card. Then play a card or tap Pass.

Action cards

  • Skip and Reverse: you go again.
  • +2 / Wild +4: opponent draws, you go again.
  • Wild: choose the next color.

Hints

  • You get 2 hints per round. A hint lights up the cards you can legally play.

Cheating 😏

  • Nothing stops you from playing a card that doesn't match. But your character gets nervous — your opponent may notice.
  • If they tap 🧐 Bluff! before making their own move, you draw 2. A cheated winning card can be called out too.

Shout DUO!

  • At 2 cards, tap DUO! before playing. Forget and your opponent can Catch! you for +2.
